Top Three Anime to Recommend

  1. Death Note
  2. Demon Slayer
  3. Attack on Titan

Death Note

This anime is a tense crime thriller about a student who finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it. At first, it's exciting; it's almost like some sort of vigilante movie, so you start to enjoy it. Then things get interesting in a very twisted way.

Demon Slayer

This is a fantasy anime about a boy who becomes a warrior to avenge his family and cure his younger sister. It's an action-packed story, but it can also be very philosophical sometimes.

Attack on Titan

This is also a fantasy anime set in a world where humanity is fighting for its survival against giant man-eating monsters called Titans. This is probably one of the most interesting pieces of literature I have consumed.
